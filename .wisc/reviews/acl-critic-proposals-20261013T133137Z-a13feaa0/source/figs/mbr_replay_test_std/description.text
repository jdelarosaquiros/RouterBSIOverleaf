Between-run variation in test routing performance. Lines show mean nDCG@10, and shaded bands span one population standard deviation on either side of the mean across three observed runs; they are not confidence intervals. Each run contributes its task-weighted mean over all test instances, with dataset scores weighted by task count. Iteration 0 denotes initialization.

Dataset=Average values are task-instance weighted (dataset means weighted by n_tasks), not an equal-weight mean over datasets.
