Baselines
314
We study retrieval, reranker, and selection base-
315
lines. The retrievers include sparse and dense re-
316
trievers (Su et al., 2026) and SkillSight, a hybrid
317
retriever. They are trained on training split and
318
frozen on validation and test splits. LLM Select is
319
a static router, and LLM Direct and LLM Oracle
320
are downstream performance baselines represent-
321
ing without skills and with ground-truth skill set,
322
respectively (Su et al., 2026). The non-parametric
323
optimization baselines are SkillDag and Skill-Pro
324
(Mi et al., 2026; Bai et al., 2026). RouterBSIinit
325
and RouterBSI refer to the cold-start and final
326
iteration performance.
RouterBSIstrict selects
327
skills only when their predicate evaluates to true.
328
RouterBSItopk are the top k skills using the sum
329
of their predicate label and the retrieval score.

5.5
Implementation Details.
331
The retrievers use either pretrained embedding
332
models and/or lexical matching, so they do not re-
333
quire training. Candidate skills are retrieved from
334
the shared external skill corpus in response to the
335
input query. The skill routing baselines are condi-
336
tioned on the top 50 retrieved skills and use some
337
optimization method to improve performance. The
338
exception is SkillDAG where it first retrieves top 5
339
skills then expands them until reaching top k. Its
340
skills are ranked based on graph depth from top 5
341
retrieved skills and retrieval score. SkillSight is the
342
default retriever. Qwen3.5-4B is the default model
343
used in our experiments. The non-parametric base-
344
lines, including ours, do not output a continuous
345
score like rerankers, so we rank them by the sum
346
of their binary label and the retrieval score, where
347
1 is the label for selected skills and 0 otherwise.
348
We use a temperature of 1 for proposing candi-
349
date predicates and 0 for evaluating predicates and
350
downstream performance. RouterBSI is optimized
351
for 10 iterations. In downstream tasks, we use k
352
= 5 skills for SkillRouter and RouterBSItopk, and
353
we set k = 10 for SkillDAG because k = 5 would
354
match the retriever’s performance.
