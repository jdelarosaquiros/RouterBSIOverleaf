RouterBSI could further improve its performance on these cases with more training iterations or relying on a human to directly edit the predicates. 6.2 Required Skill Retention RouterBSI retains more required skills from initial top 50 retrieval than other baselines as we reduce the max k of skills loaded into LLM Agent. For instance, Figure 3 shows that RouterBSI retained 90.5% of the required skills in top 5 from the top 50 skills retrieved, whereas the retention of baselines ranged from 59.5% to 83.9% on average across retrievers. Despite its low retention in top 5 of 59.5%, SkillDAG has an average 16.1% more recall of required skills in top 50 than the base retrievers with an average recall score of 96.86% compared to 83.45%, respectively. The higher coverage in top 50 of SkillDAG’s expansion plus the retrieval score can be a better alternative for initial retrieval than relying on the retrievers alone. Combining the higher coverage of SkillDAG in top 50 with better retention of RouterBSI could lead to better routing performance. 6.3 Downstream Performance Figure 4 demonstrates that combining RouterBSI selected skills with some additional retrieved skills leads to slightly higher mean downstream performance as RouterBSItop5’s average performance across retrievers is 2.5% higher than RouterBSIstrict.
