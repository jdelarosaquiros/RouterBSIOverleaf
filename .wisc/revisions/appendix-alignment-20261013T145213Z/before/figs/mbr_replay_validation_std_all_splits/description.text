Routing-score trajectories by evaluation split. Lines show mean nDCG@10 across three runs; shading denotes ±1 population standard deviation, not a confidence interval. Each run is summarized by its task-weighted mean across tasks in the relevant split/evaluation, with dataset scores weighted by task count. Positions 0 and 11 are initialization and final snapshots. At intermediate iterations, training is evaluated prequentially, validation uses ledger-accepted candidates, and test uses accepted candidates; the panels therefore have different evaluation stages.

Dataset=Average values are task-instance weighted (dataset means weighted by n_tasks), not an equal-weight mean over datasets.
